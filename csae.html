Child Safety Standards

Zero Tolerance Policy

NearMate has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, exploitation, or any content or behavior that harms or endangers minors.

Any account found violating these standards may be removed, reported, and permanently restricted from using NearMate.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexualization of minors
  • Grooming, coercion, or exploitation of minors
  • Sharing, requesting, or promoting sexual content involving minors
  • Any attempt to contact minors for harmful, exploitative, or sexual purposes
  • Content that facilitates, encourages, or normalizes child abuse or exploitation

Safety Measures

  • Users can report profiles, messages, and inappropriate behavior directly in the app.
  • Users can block other users to prevent unwanted contact.
  • Reports are reviewed and may result in account restrictions, suspension, or removal.
  • NearMate may remove violating content and accounts when abuse or exploitation is detected.
  • NearMate may cooperate with law enforcement or relevant authorities where legally required.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter content, behavior, or communication that may involve child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or harm to minors, please report it immediately using the in-app reporting tools.

You may also contact our support team directly.
