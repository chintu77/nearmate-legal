# NearMate Child Safety Standards NearMate has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit: • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) • Grooming or exploitation of minors • Sexualization of minors • Any content that promotes, depicts, or facilitates abuse of children Safety Measures: • User reporting tools • User blocking tools • Content moderation and review • Account suspension and removal for violations • Cooperation with law enforcement when legally required If you encounter content or behavior that may violate these standards, please report it immediately through the in-app reporting features or contact: [support@nearmatechat.com](mailto:support@nearmatechat.com) NearMate is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations.
