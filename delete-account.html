Delete Your NearMate Account

NearMate allows users to permanently delete their account and associated personal data directly from within the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the NearMate app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your password if requested.
  6. Confirm account deletion.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Profile information
  • Profile and gallery photos
  • Authentication account
  • User preferences and settings
  • Connections and social graph data
  • Messages and user-generated content associated with your account
  • Other personal information used for app functionality

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained for a limited period for fraud prevention, abuse investigations, security monitoring, legal compliance, and system recovery purposes where required by law.

Need Help?

If you are unable to access your account or experience issues with account deletion, contact our support team.

support@nearmatechat.com
