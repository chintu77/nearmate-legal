# NearMate Account Deletion At NearMate, we respect your privacy and provide users with the ability to permanently delete their account and associated personal data. ## How to Delete Your Account You can delete your account directly from within the NearMate app: 1. Open the NearMate app. 2. Navigate to **Profile**. 3. Tap **Settings**. 4. Select **Delete Account**. 5. Confirm your password (if required). 6. Confirm account deletion. Once completed, your account deletion request will be processed and you will be signed out of the application. ## What Data Is Deleted When your account is deleted, NearMate will permanently remove: * Account profile information * Profile photo and gallery images * Authentication credentials * User preferences and settings * Connection and social graph data * Messages and user-generated content associated with your account * Other personal information stored by NearMate for app functionality ## Data Retention Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for: * Security and fraud prevention * Abuse and policy violation investigations * Legal or regulatory compliance * System backup and recovery processes Any retained information is handled according to our Privacy Policy and applicable laws. ## Need Assistance? If you are unable to access your account or experience issues with account deletion, please contact our support team: **Email:** [support@nearmatechat.com](mailto:support@nearmatechat.com) ## Privacy Policy For more information about how NearMate collects, uses, and protects user data, please review our Privacy Policy: https://nearmatechat.com/privacy.html Last Updated: June 2026
